Why moods, not a playlist

Every office has its own soundtrack, whether anyone plans it or not — the hum of the aircon, a call bleeding through someone's headset, the particular quiet right before a deadline. Nobody sits down and curates that; it just happens depending on what the day looks like.

This page turns that instinct into a small design object: four moods instead of infinite choice, each one built for a different shape of the workday — pick whichever matches how things are actually going.
